      [PRIVACY] > policy

      Effective 10 May 2026. We will publicly note any material change at least 30 days before it takes effect.

      > This policy describes what QloakMail collects, what we deliberately do not collect, and what happens to the information we have to handle in order to deliver mail. It applies to the webmail at qloak.me, the SMTP / IMAP servers operated under that domain, and the corresponding Tor v3 hidden service.

      [WHAT WE DO NOT COLLECT]

      • Your IP address. The API is configured to suppress access logs entirely. Rate-limit keys are derived as HMAC(IP, per-process-secret) and discarded when the process restarts; the secret itself is never written to disk.
      • User agents, browser fingerprints, device IDs. We do not run analytics, ad pixels, font probes, or JavaScript that profiles your device.
      • Message contents. We physically cannot read them. Every inbound message is encrypted to your OpenPGP public key before it touches the mailstore. Outbound mail composed in the webmail is encrypted in your browser to internal recipients.
      • Search queries. Search runs entirely in your browser against an IndexedDB index of already-decrypted previews. Queries never reach the server.
      • Behavioural data. No tracking cookies, no event pings, no third-party scripts.
      • Payment identifiers (where the service is paid). We accept anonymous payment methods where available and never tie a payment to a mailbox identity.

      [WHAT WE STORE]

      • Your email address, so mail can be routed to you.
      • Your OpenPGP public key, used to encrypt incoming mail at the moment of receipt.
      • An Argon2id-AES-GCM-encrypted blob of your private key. Only you can decrypt it — with your password or your one-time recovery code. The server never holds the decryption key.
      • An SRP-6a verifier for password authentication. The verifier cannot be inverted to recover the password.
      • The minimum SMTP routing metadata a mail server has to handle: envelope sender, envelope recipient, message size, received-at timestamp. No MTA on earth can avoid these — they are how mail is delivered. The original Subject is encrypted inside the PGP body; the outer envelope reads only [encrypted].
      • Encrypted message blobs in your mailbox until you delete them. Deleted mail is purged from the mailstore on the next housekeeping pass.

      [RETENTION]

      • Mail you keep: stored encrypted indefinitely, until you delete it or close the account.
      • Mail you delete: purged within 30 days; in-flight backups and disaster-recovery snapshots are encrypted and rotated on the same schedule.
      • Account record: deleted on account closure. We retain only what is necessary to honour outstanding obligations (e.g. an unfinished bounce queue) and even that is purged within 30 days of closure.
      • Logs: the only logs we keep are postfix / dovecot delivery logs that go to a volatile in-memory journal (lost on reboot). Nothing is shipped off the host.

      [SECURITY MEASURES]

      • End-to-end encryption via OpenPGP (RFC 4880 / RFC 3156) with keys generated client-side at signup.
      • SRP-6a authentication so the password never crosses the wire.
      • Inbound and outbound TLS — STARTTLS on port 25, modern AEAD ciphers only, MTA-STS in enforce mode for senders that support it. DANE/TLSA pinning where DNSSEC is in place.
      • Hardened web surface — strict Content-Security-Policy, Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy, Cross-Origin-Resource-Policy, Cross-Origin-Embedder-Policy, Permissions-Policy, Subresource-Integrity-pinned crypto libraries.
      • Idle auto-lock after 15 minutes of inactivity in the webmail, dropping the in-memory private key.
      • Clipboard auto-clear 30 seconds after copying sensitive material (recovery codes, .onion address).
      • Container isolation with no-new-privileges and minimal Linux capabilities; database and cache tiers reachable only on the internal Docker network.

      [ON YOUR DEVICE]

      The webmail uses localStorage / sessionStorage for your session token, your public key, the SRP/Argon2 parameters, and — only if you choose a "stay signed in" duration — an encrypted private key blob bound to that expiry. An IndexedDB index holds decrypted previews of messages you have opened, so search can run locally. All of this is cleared on sign-out, and the encrypted-blob storage is keyed to your password — without it, the data is mathematically useless.

      [COOKIES]

      QloakMail does not set tracking cookies. It uses the browser storage above (localStorage / sessionStorage / IndexedDB) for functional purposes only. There are no third-party cookies, analytics cookies, or marketing cookies anywhere on the site.
