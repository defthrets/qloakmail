      [PRIVACY] > policy

      Last updated: 10 May 2026

      What we don't collect

      • IP addresses — never written to a persistent log; transient rate-limit keys are hashed with a per-process secret and discarded on restart.
      • User agents, fingerprints, devices.
      • Message contents. We can't — they're encrypted to you before they reach our storage.
      • Search queries. Search runs locally in your browser; queries never reach the server.
      • Behavioural analytics, cookies for tracking, third-party scripts. None used.

      What we keep

      • Your email address, so we can route mail to you.
      • An encrypted blob of your private key — only you can decrypt it, with your password or recovery code.
      • Your public key, used to encrypt incoming mail before it touches storage.
      • The minimum routing metadata required to deliver mail (envelope sender / recipient / size / received-at). No MTA can avoid these.

      Storage on your device

      The webmail uses localStorage / sessionStorage for your session token (and, if you opted in, an encrypted private-key blob bound to a duration), plus an IndexedDB index of decrypted message previews so search works locally. All of this clears on sign-out.

      Disclosure

      We respond to legitimate legal process to the limited extent required by law. The defence is what we don't have: we cannot produce plaintext mail, IP histories, or behavioural profiles, because we never collected them.

      Contact

      Questions or requests: privacy@qloak.me.
